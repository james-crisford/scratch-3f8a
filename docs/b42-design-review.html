Bird's eye. White rim halo + dark cup interior + flagstick centre.

Inspecting the cup. Wall gradient sells depth.

4. Side profile (5°)

5. Diagonal walk-by (40°)

Off-axis view. Flag visibility check.

6. Wide putt-view

Standard putting POV. Ball foreground, hole + flag in distance.

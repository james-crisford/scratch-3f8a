The putting pose — 4 technical diagrams

Right-handed user, both hands on the phone, in the putting grip. Annotated SVG showing which way each axis of the phone points relative to the target line, the user's body, and the ground. Per James's 11-point confirmation 2026-06-03.

1. Top-down view

looking straight down at the floor — target is at the TOP of the frame
Phone held with long axis perpendicular to target line. The long edge that's closest to the hole = the "putt face". The other long edge points back toward the user. Both hands grip the bottom edge of the phone (in 3D, this is where the lightning port is — facing user's chest).

2. Behind the user (target-line POV)

camera is behind user, looking forward to the hole — shows the tilt
Camera lens points at user's feet (orange arrow). Top of phone angles down toward ground. The "putt face" (long edge facing target) points INTO the page. The hole + flag are in the distance along the target line.

3. Side-on — target's view of user

target is OUT of the frame to the RIGHT; user faces right
Profile view from the side. The phone is held tilted forward at ~60° from vertical (matching natural putter lie angle). Top of the phone (camera end) aims down at the ground. Both hands grip the bottom edge (closer to the user's chest).

4. What the user sees

user's downward glance at the phone screen
What it looks like from the user's point of view, looking down at the screen at a slight angle while holding the phone in the putting pose. AR scene + hole + flag visible. HUD shows the build version. Single bottom hint: "Hold to address, swing to putt". Both hands wrap the bottom edge of the phone.

What each option changes

  • Option 2 — Soft AO shadow ring. Replaces the hard-edged AO line at the cup rim with a soft radial falloff (1cm wide gradient from 0.55 opacity at rim → 0 opacity at +1cm out). Sells "this cup is recessed" much more strongly than the hard ring. Render cost: identical (same vertex count, just a different fragment colour). Time to implement: 20 min.
  • Option 3 — Striped flagstick. Replaces the uniform white pole with 5 alternating red/white bands (like USGA marker tape on real flagsticks). Each band is ~14 cm tall. Reads as "real golf course" instead of "abstract pole". Render cost: small (one more material per band, or a single banded texture). Time: 30 min.
  • Option 4 — Fabric flag with stripes. Triangle flag gets a horizontal red/white stripe pattern (like pin flags at major tournaments) + a slight wave deformation so it doesn't look paper-flat. Vertex animation gives a gentle 0.5 Hz flutter. Time: 45 min.

All three are additive — they can ship together as one B43, OR you can pick any subset. None touch the cup geometry (still the locked B40 recessed cylinder). None reintroduce the SimpleMaterial darkening risk — every change uses UnlitMaterial or static texture lookup.

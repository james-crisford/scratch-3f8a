10 Hole Design Options

Side-by-side comparison of every direction we could take the hole render. Column 1 is what's currently shipping in B42. Columns 2–10 are alternatives — pick any combination, or use one as a starting point and we iterate from there.
Shipping

01. B42 Baseline

Current spec
White rim + 3D recessed cup + white flagstick + red triangle flag. Locked at B40 materials. What you'll see in build 43.
Tweak

02. Soft AO Shadow

+ depth perception
Replaces hard AO ring with a radial gradient (dark at rim → fade at +1cm). Sells "recessed" much more strongly. Identical render cost.
Tweak

03. Striped Flagstick

+ course-realism
5 alternating red/white bands on the pole, mimicking USGA marker tape. Reads as "real golf course".
Tweak

04. Fabric Striped Flag

+ realism
Tournament-pin pattern — 3 horizontal red bands on white with fabric noise. Could pair with #03.
Bold

05. Glowing Cup Rim ⚡

"lights up"
Bright cyan halo glowing around the rim. UnlitMaterial at full brightness — looks like a tracker beacon. Could ALSO pulse if we want animation.
Bold

06. Pulse Rim (peak frame)

animated halo
Same as #05 but breathing — soft 1 Hz pulse from 20% → 70% opacity. Shown here at peak.
Bold

07. Bullseye Target Rings

putting practice aid
3 concentric rings around the cup (50cm, 1m, 2m radii) to gauge near-misses. Faint white outlines. Genuinely useful for practice.
Bold

08. Premium Gold Rim

luxury aesthetic
Gold rim ring + matte black flagstick + small gold ferrule near cup. Reads premium / private-course.
Bold

09. Minimalist Void

stripped down
No white rim halo, no concentric layers. Just a pure dark recess + a slim flagstick. Most "design app" feel.
Bold

10. Deluxe Combo

everything stacked
Soft AO + striped flagstick + striped flag + subtle glow ring + bullseye targets. Maxed-out visual fidelity. ~3 hr code work.

Option 8 — Premium Gold (depth-emphasised)

Showing the gold rim from FIVE camera angles plus a side-cutaway diagram so the cup's 3D recess is unambiguous. The actual app will render the same geometry — we control how steep the user holds the phone but ARKit reports the real surface, so the cup IS physically 8 cm deep in the floor regardless of view angle.

Five rendered viewpoints

1. Top-down (overhead)

Gold rim halo + dark cup interior + tiny black bottom visible at centre. The gold "ring" is the rim seen from above.

2. 35° putting stance

Typical phone-aim angle. Cup mouth foreshortened to an ellipse — but you can see partial wall depth + shadow.

3. 55° steep angle ★

Best 3D-read angle. Cup mouth opens up clearly — you can see WALL going down + bottom at the back. This is what the user will see if they tilt the phone down.

4. 75° near-overhead close-up

Looking almost straight down into the cup from close range. Dark wall gradient + bottom black. Unmistakably recessed.

5. Low grazing (12°)

Almost flat to the floor. Rim becomes a profile slice. Flagstick + shadow dominate.

6. Side cutaway (impossible IRL)

Camera placed below floor level for design-review only. Shows the physical cylindrical recess + bottom + flagstick base.

Cross-section diagram (mechanical drawing)

Cross-section through the centre. Floor is the horizontal line at Y=0. The cup walls extend DOWN 8 cm into the floor. The flagstick rises 70 cm up from the cup base. Gold rim sits flush at the floor level; gold ferrule wraps the flagstick base where it emerges.

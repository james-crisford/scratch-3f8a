PuttingLab — Hole Iteration Viewport

Live Three.js render of the B40 hole geometry. Same dimensions + materials the app uses. Six views so you can sanity-check what the camera will see.

1. Top-Down

Bird's-eye. White halo, dark cup interior, flagstick centred.

2. Putting Stance (35°)

Camera at typical phone-aim angle. Depth into the cup should read here.

3. Close-Up Hover (20°)

Inspecting the cup. Wall gradient + AO at the rim sells the depth.

4. Side Profile (5°)

Almost flat to floor. Tests whether the rim+wall reads from a low angle.

5. Diagonal Walk-By (45°)

Mid-walk-by angle. Flag visibility check.

6. Behind the Ball (look toward hole)

Standard putting POV — ball in foreground, hole + flagstick beyond.

B40 geometry (locked):

• Rim disc: 12.9 cm dia, white UnlitMaterial, flush at floor

• Cup wall: 10.8 cm dia × 8 cm depth, white UnlitMaterial, goes DOWN into floor

• Cup bottom: 10.3 cm dia disc, dark warm-gray SimpleMaterial, at −8 cm

• Flagstick: 1.5 cm dia × 70 cm tall, white UnlitMaterial

• Flag: triangle 15 cm × 10 cm, red UnlitMaterial, mid-pole +X side

• Ball (View 6): 4.27 cm dia sphere, white UnlitMaterial, 1.49 m from hole

PuttingLab B40 — Hole Design Mockup

Three views of the proposed B40 hole render based on Gemini's CAC00F findings. The defining change vs B39: the cup is now a real 3D recess (cylindrical wall going 8 cm into the floor with a dark bottom), and all white parts use UnlitMaterial so they render visibly white regardless of ARKit's lighting estimation. Triangle flag replaces the rigid 2D rectangle.

View 1 — Top-Down

Pure top-down. Reads as a regulation white-rimmed cup with a dark shadowed interior. The 12.9 cm white halo (UnlitMaterial) is unambiguous against the wood floor. Flagstick is the small white dot at centre. Flag is foreshortened to a sliver.

View 2 — 35° Camera Angle

Typical putting-stance view (~35° down). Depth into the cup is visible via the white wall transitioning to dark bottom, plus the AO crescent at the far rim. Triangle flag now reads as a real flag, not a rigid rectangle.

View 3 — Close-Up Crop

Tight crop, looking straight into the cup. The radial gradient on the wall (white → mid-gray → dark) sells the depth illusion. RealityKit will do this naturally because the UnlitMaterial wall is recessed 8 cm — the radial fade is real geometry + ambient light, not a baked gradient. Mock-up is showing what the eye will read.

What's different from B39

  • UnlitMaterial on rim + wall + flagstick. White stays white regardless of indoor lighting.
  • Cylinder wall instead of 3 stacked planes. Real 8 cm recess → real depth.
  • Dark bottom disc at the base of the cylinder is what the eye sees through the opening.
  • Triangle flag via MeshDescriptor (both faces) — reads as flag from any angle.
  • Plane overlay filter: floors only, ≥ 1 m². Removes Gemini-flagged secondary planes.
  • deviceInfo + materialApplied + planeClassification events for future debugging.

Material spec (B40)

  • Rim disc — UnlitMaterial, RGB(255, 255, 255), 12.9 cm dia, 0.5 mm above plane
  • Wall — UnlitMaterial, RGB(255, 255, 255), 10.8 cm dia × 8 cm height, top at plane
  • Bottom — SimpleMaterial dark warm gray RGB(25, 20, 15), 10.3 cm dia, at -8 cm
  • Flagstick — UnlitMaterial white, 1.5 cm dia × 70 cm tall
  • Flag — UnlitMaterial RGB(230, 25, 25), 15 cm × 10 cm right triangle

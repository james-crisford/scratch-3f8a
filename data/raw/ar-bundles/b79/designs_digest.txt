--- algorithm:
FRAME + CONVENTIONS
- ARKit world frame: right-handed, +Y up. World origin = phone pose at session start (floor is at large negative y, e.g. -0.853 this session). Yaw convention used throughout the codebase for floor vectors: yaw(v) = atan2(v.x, v.z); increasing yaw = CCW viewed from above; simd_quatf(angle: yaw, axis: +Y) maps local +Z to (sin yaw, 0, cos yaw). Verified against the session log: atan2(aim.x, aim.z) = 160.9005° vs logged aim_yaw_deg 160.90.
- Perpendiculars of a horizontal unit aim vector a = (ax, 0, az):
  - leftPerp(a)  = up × a = ( az, 0, -ax)   ("left" for an observer walking ball→hole)
  - rightPerp(a) = a × up = (-az, 0,  ax)   (this is the existing code's `perp` at ARPlacementView:3784 and BallRollAnimator:119 — it is RIGHT of aim, the root of the mirror finding)
  Define leftPerp(of:) ONCE (in StanceGeometry) as the single source of truth; the renderer perp fix (roll-render-mirror-flip) should adopt the same helper. This change consumes it independently — no coupling to the physics/sign fix.

INPUTS
- ball b, hole h: raycast floor world positions (b.y IS the floor height at the ball).
- stance: StanceGeometry (gains heightMetres stored field) — shoulderWidthMetres = 0.245·H, footHalfSpreadMetres = shoulderWidth/2, handToBallMetres = putterLen·cos(70°) ≈ 0.295 m @34in.
- handedness: UserProfile.Handedness (already persisted; threaded to all 7 call sites which all hold the profile: ARPlacementView lines 780, 1907, 1930, 1947, 4810, 4830, 4844).
- Tunables: liftMetres = 0.010, ballForwardMetres = 0.040 (clamp [0, 0.08]), toeOutRadians = 7° (clamp [0°, 12°]).

STEP 1 — aim. aimVec = (h.x-b.x, 0, h.z-b.z); aimLen = |aimVec|; guard aimLen > 0.01 and all components finite, else return nil BEFORE clearing old markers (preserves the B79 co-located-transient guard ordering). a = aimVec/aimLen.

STEP 2 — side selection. sideSign s = +1 if handedness == .right, -1 if .left. sideVec = s · leftPerp(a). A right-handed golfer stands on the LEFT of the aim vector (derivation in why_correct; confirmed by the session video — James's real feet were left of the line). Settings handedness is the ONLY input that places markers. Camera inference (below) is validator/telemetry only.

STEP 3 — stance-line setback (height-scaled, perpendicular to the line).
setbackMetres = handToBallMetres + 0.04 · heightMetres.
Derivation: (a) putter-lie trig pins the hands: a conforming 34 in putter at 70°-from-vertical lie puts the grip 0.864·cos70° ≈ 0.295 m horizontally from the ball (StanceGeometry's existing handToBallMetres — finally consumed; fix its doc comment to say "perpendicular to the target line", and note the "70° from vertical (USGA)" label is the dubious convention — conventional putter lie is ~70° from horizontal — but cos(70°) is the correct horizontal projection either way, so 0.295 m stands). (b) eyes-over-ball posture: ball under the eyes; hip-to-eye ≈ 0.406·H, hip-hinge ≈ 40°, hips counterbalanced back ≈ 0.05·H ⇒ toe-line-to-ball ≈ H·(0.406·sin40° − 0.05) ≈ 0.20–0.22·H. The toes sit a height-scaled step behind the hand projection, hence +0.04·H. Defaults: H = 1.78 m → setback = 0.295 + 0.071 = 0.367 m (= 0.206·H, inside the 0.35–0.45 m empirical band); H = 1.70 → 0.363; session H = 1.85 → 0.369. Setback varies weakly with height because the putter pins the hands — physically right (shorter players stand more upright).

STEP 4 — stance line center, with ball slightly forward of center.
center = b + sideVec·setback − a·ballForwardMetres;   center.y = b.y + liftMetres.
Ball position choice: slightly forward of stance center by 0.04 m (≈ one golf-ball width, 42.7 mm). Justification: standard putting doctrine (Pelz et al.) puts the ball 1–2 ball-widths ahead of center so contact happens fractionally on the upstroke for true roll; dead-center is the conservative alternative but "center to slightly forward" resolves to slightly-forward in essentially all modern putting instruction. Implemented by shifting the FEET back along −aim (the ball anchor never moves).

STEP 5 — feet spread ALONG the aim direction.
leadFoot  = center + a·footHalfSpread   (toward the hole)
trailFoot = center − a·footHalfSpread
Both y = b.y + lift. Spread = shoulder width (lead-to-trail distance = 2·footHalfSpread = 0.245·H), axis = aim, NOT perp (the B77/B79 bug applied the spread across the line).

STEP 6 — foot orientation: long axes point AT the line (perpendicular to aim), toes toward the line.
Mesh stays generatePlane(width: 0.10 (footWid), depth: 0.24 (footLen)) — long axis on local +Z. We want local +Z along −sideVec (from the golfer's toes toward the ball line):
baseFootYaw = aimYaw − s·(π/2), where aimYaw = atan2(a.x, a.z).
(Check: (sin baseFootYaw, 0, cos baseFootYaw) = −s·leftPerp exactly — verified numerically to 5 dp.)
Optional toe-out (toes flare apart, lead toward target):
leadFootYaw  = baseFootYaw + s·toeOut
trailFootYaw = baseFootYaw − s·toeOut
orientation = simd_quatf(angle: yaw, axis: (0,1,0)).

--- why_correct:
SIDE DERIVATION (RH golfer stands LEFT of the aim vector) — bird's-eye diagram, looking straight down (along −Y). In ARKit's right-handed +Y-up frame, viewing from above with aim drawn up-page gives a standard map view (verified: up × aim = "page-left" = the left hand of an observer walking ball→hole).

            HOLE
              ▲
              │ aim
   lead foot ▭│        ← lead foot toward hole
   (toes →)   │
        ●─────┤BALL    ● = ball, one ball-width ahead of stance center
   trail foot▭│
              │
   GOLFER     │
   (left side)│
              ▼ (behind ball)

A right-handed golfer swings the putter with the target on his LEFT: he faces the line square-on, and his left-hand side points along aim. Formally: let f = the golfer's facing direction (from his chest toward the line, horizontal). "Left of facing f" = up × f. Target-on-his-left means up × f = aim, which solves to f = aim × up = rightPerp(aim). He stands offset from the ball OPPOSITE to his facing: golferSide = ball − f·setback = ball + leftPerp(aim)·setback. So RH ⇒ LEFT of aim (up × aim side); LH mirrors to rightPerp. Cross-checks: (1) classic down-the-line camera footage (behind the ball looking at the hole) shows RH golfers on the left of the frame's target line; (2) GROUND TRUTH from today's video — James (right-handed) had his real feet on the LEFT of the ball-hole line, straddled by the wrongly-placed markers; (3) the algebra up × f = aim has a unique solution among the two perpendiculars — facing the other way would put the target on his right (the left-handed stance).

CROSS-PRODUCT SIGNS VERIFIED NUMERICALLY: up × a for a = (ax, 0, az) is (1·az − 0, 0 − 0, 0 − 1·ax) = (az, 0, −ax). Sanity probe with a = (0, 0, −1) (the session-start camera forward): up × a = (−1, 0, 0) = −X, and ARKit's start convention is +X to the camera's right, so −X is indeed LEFT looking along −Z. The existing code's perp = (−az, 0, ax) = a × up = (+1, 0, 0) = RIGHT — which is exactly why the current ± spread straddles and why the roll renders mirrored (separate finding).
